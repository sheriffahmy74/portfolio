// featured · founder project

Lamma لمّة

A group-outings app for Egypt. Pick an outing, book a seat in the group that fits you, pay, then meet your group in a chat before you meet them in person.

Discovery

Find an outing that fits you

Categories, personal suggestions and search filters for city, group size, all-in price, date and time of day.

Groups & booking

One outing, many groups

Each outing has groups for different audiences. Seat capacity is enforced on the server inside a row lock, so two people can never take the last seat.

Payments & wallet

Cards, wallets and InstaPay

Payments go through Paymob. A booking is confirmed only by the HMAC-verified webhook, never by the app reporting its own success.

Realtime chat

Meet the group first

Group chat on Supabase Realtime with reactions, mentions and deleted messages. Membership follows the booking.

Arabic-first · RTL

Built for Arabic, works in English

Every string exists in both languages and the whole layout mirrors for RTL. Notifications are composed and localized on the server.
